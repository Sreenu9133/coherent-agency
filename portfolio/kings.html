Purpose-built for developers, BoostSecurity integrates directly into CI/CD pipelines and dev workflows, providing automated security scanning, policy enforcement, and risk prioritization, all without slowing down the development process.

The Challenges

BoostSecurity operates in one of the most competitive and technically demanding SaaS categories: developer security tooling. The existing website needed a ground-up redesign that could communicate a complex, multi-layered platform to both engineering leaders evaluating tools and developers who would use them daily.

  • Translating a deeply technical DevSecOps platform into a clear, compelling web narrative that resonated with both security teams and developers.
  • Designing and building custom interactive components, including an animated architecture diagram and a product tour with spotlight overlays, tooltips, and responsive layout logic, entirely within Webflow embeds.
  • Establishing a scalable design system with strict brand guidelines including a blue brand palette, Nexa Bold and Comfortaa Bold typography, and JetBrains Mono for code-level elements.
  • Building a comprehensive CMS architecture for blog content, resource pages, and product documentation that the BoostSecurity team could manage independently.
  • Implementing a micro-interactions system across the entire site to create a polished, premium browsing experience without compromising performance.

DevSecOps platforms compete against well-funded incumbents with substantial design budgets. Our Webflow design and development capabilities were directly tested by this level of complexity and visual expectation.

Our Development Approach

We executed a complete website redesign for BoostSecurity on Webflow, combining custom component engineering, brand system implementation, and CMS architecture into an ongoing, evolving engagement.

  • Redesigned and rebuilt the entire BoostSecurity website from the ground up in Webflow, implementing a cohesive blue brand system (#4087C7 primary) with Nexa Bold, Comfortaa Bold, and JetBrains Mono typography.
  • Engineered a custom animated architecture section that visually communicates BoostSecurity's platform capabilities through layered, interactive motion
  • Built a sophisticated interactive product tour featuring spotlight overlays, contextual tooltips, and fully responsive layout, all scoped for Webflow embed deployment
  • Developed a site-wide micro-interactions system covering hover states, scroll-triggered animations, and transition sequences for a premium user experience
  • Architected CMS collections for blog listings, blog post templates, and resource content, enabling the BoostSecurity team to publish and manage content without developer involvement
  • Delivered key pages including Terms of Service, Contact, Blog Listing, Blog Post Template, and Request a Demo, each following strict copy and formatting rules

Custom interactive components inside Webflow embeds represent some of the most technically challenging work in the Webflow ecosystem. Our SaaS website design expertise is built for exactly this type of engineering-grade implementation.
